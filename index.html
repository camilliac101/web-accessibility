Welcome to Your Guide on Web Accessibility

This website was created to help explain what web accessibility is, why it matters, and how anyone designing or building a website can improve their pages to work for more people.

Whether you're a student, a beginner in web design, or just someone interested in making the internet more inclusive, this site walks you through the basics in a simple, clear way. You’ll learn about real-world challenges, easy fixes, and even test your knowledge in a fun quiz.

Why This Site Exists

Over a billion people around the world live with some form of disability. Many rely on assistive technologies or adapted tools to browse the internet. Making websites accessible helps everyone — not just those with disabilities — by improving usability, readability, and design for all users.
