What Is Web Accessibility?

Web accessibility means making websites usable by as many people as possible, including those with disabilities. It’s about ensuring that all users—whether they’re using a screen reader, navigating with a keyboard, or dealing with visual impairments—can access the same information and perform the same actions online.

Why Accessibility Matters

The internet is a key part of education, communication, and business. When a website isn’t accessible, it excludes people from participating fully in digital spaces. Making your site accessible ensures that nobody is left out because of the way they interact with technology.

Who It Helps

Web accessibility benefits people with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor disabilities. For example, someone who is blind may use a screen reader to read webpage content aloud. Others may rely on keyboard navigation if they can’t use a mouse.

Did You Know?

Over 1 billion people worldwide live with a disability. Accessible design isn’t just helpful—it’s essential to equal access.
